Refine the **existing desktop low-fidelity homepage** rather than redesigning it from scratch.

The current overall information architecture is good and should be preserved.

Keep the existing section order:

**Header
→ Global Navigation
→ Hero / Global Search
→ Core Service Shortcuts
→ Community + Welfare & Support Information
→ Hospitals & Professional Institutions + Assistive Devices
→ Footer**

This iteration should improve **information hierarchy, spacing, section rhythm, clarity, accessibility, and consistency** while preserving the restrained low-fidelity character of the current design.

All user-facing UI text must remain in Korean.

---

## 1. Preserve the Existing Strengths

Keep the following characteristics of the current design:

* Search-centered homepage
* Simple global navigation
* Four clear core-service entry points
* List-based Community preview
* Structured Welfare & Support Information preview
* Text-based status labels
* Simple institution discovery
* Information-oriented assistive-device discovery
* Minimal use of cards
* Restrained visual styling
* Simple footer
* No AI-focused functionality
* No personalization
* No location-based features
* No promotional content

Do not redesign these areas into dashboard widgets or large decorative cards.

---

## 2. Simplify Search Duplication

The homepage already has a prominent global search field in the Hero.

On the homepage, reduce unnecessary duplication between the Header search action and the Hero search.

Prefer the Hero search field as the primary search entry point.

The Header should remain simple and primarily contain:

* Logo
* Login
* Sign Up

Do not weaken the Hero search experience.

The global search architecture may still exist on other pages later, but this homepage does not need two equally visible search entry points.

---

## 3. Refine the Hero

Keep the current search-centered Hero concept.

Do not introduce imagery, illustrations, banners, gradients, or promotional content.

Improve the Hero by:

* reducing unnecessary empty vertical or horizontal space,
* creating a clearer relationship between the headline and search field,
* keeping the headline concise and easy to scan,
* giving the search field clear visual priority,
* and maintaining comfortable whitespace without making the Hero feel unfinished.

Keep the general message:

**필요한 장애 관련 정보를 찾아보세요**

The Hero should feel like a functional discovery area, not a marketing landing page.

---

## 4. Refine Core Service Shortcuts

Keep these four services:

* 커뮤니티
* 복지·지원정보
* 병원·전문기관
* 보조기기

Preserve the simple horizontal structure.

Replace colorful emoji-style icons with **simple neutral monochrome placeholder icons** appropriate for a low-fidelity wireframe.

Icons should support the text, not dominate it.

Do not make these items into large floating cards.

Keep each service name clearly visible and retain short supporting descriptions where useful.

The UI architecture should remain disability-neutral even though the MVP sample content focuses on hearing disabilities.

---

## 5. Preserve the Community List Structure

The current Community section works well as a simple list.

Keep:

* post title,
* category,
* date,
* optional comment count,
* and `커뮤니티 더보기`.

Do not convert every post into an individual card.

Maintain strong scanability.

The Community section represents **user-generated experiences and discussions**, so preserve a subtle distinction between this content and official structured information.

---

## 6. Preserve and Clarify Welfare Information

Keep the existing structured list approach for Welfare & Support Information.

Continue showing concise information such as:

* program name,
* eligibility,
* application period,
* textual status.

Keep textual status labels such as:

* 접수 중
* 상시 운영
* 접수 예정

Do not communicate status through color alone.

Strengthen the sense that this is **structured or official information**, while the Community section represents user-generated content.

Use hierarchy, labels, typography, and structure rather than heavy decorative styling to communicate this distinction.

---

## 7. Refine Hospitals & Professional Institutions

Keep the current simple institution-discovery structure and `기관 찾기` CTA.

Do not add:

* nearby institutions,
* maps,
* location detection,
* ratings,
* booking,
* or other functionality that is not part of the current MVP.

Where general interface copy describes the section, avoid making the permanent UI architecture unnecessarily specific to hearing disabilities.

For example, prefer broader interface language such as:

**필요한 진료·재활·전문기관 정보를 찾아보세요**

The actual sample institutions may still focus on hearing-related services because the MVP content currently targets users with hearing disabilities.

This distinction is important:

**MVP content may be hearing-disability focused, but the UI architecture should remain extensible to other disability groups.**

---

## 8. Preserve the Assistive Device Information Style

Keep this section informational rather than commercial.

Maintain simple product information such as:

* product name,
* manufacturer,
* product type,
* representative information.

Do not add:

* prices,
* purchase buttons,
* shopping-cart behavior,
* star ratings,
* promotional product photography,
* discount labels,
* or e-commerce patterns.

The section should feel like a product information database, not an online store.

Keep the `제품 찾기` CTA.

---

## 9. Improve Section Hierarchy and Page Rhythm

The current page uses repeated two-column structures for:

* Community + Welfare
* Hospitals + Assistive Devices

Preserve the efficient desktop layout, but improve the visual rhythm so the page does not feel like a repetitive document grid.

Use primarily:

* spacing,
* typography hierarchy,
* alignment,
* subtle dividers,
* section grouping,
* and content density

to create clearer separation between major information groups.

Do NOT solve this by wrapping every section in a large rounded card.

Avoid unnecessary:

* shadows,
* gradients,
* pills,
* badges,
* background decorations,
* oversized icons,
* or floating containers.

The result should remain calm and information-focused.

---

## 10. Accessibility Refinement

Preserve accessibility as a core requirement.

Ensure the refined structure supports:

* clear heading hierarchy,
* readable text,
* visible interactive controls,
* meaningful text labels,
* logical keyboard navigation order,
* visible focus-state design potential,
* status communication beyond color,
* sufficient spacing around interactive elements,
* and text enlargement without fragile fixed-height containers.

Do not reduce text size merely to create more whitespace.

Do not rely on subtle low-contrast gray text for important information.

---

## 11. Maintain Low-Fidelity Scope

This is still a **low-fidelity structural iteration**.

Do not turn the homepage into a polished high-fidelity design yet.

Do not finalize:

* brand colors,
* exact typography scale,
* exact spacing tokens,
* border-radius system,
* detailed iconography,
* shadows,
* illustrations,
* animations,
* or visual branding.

Use neutral styling and focus on structure.

---

## 12. Do Not Add New Features

Do not add any functionality that is not already represented in the current homepage.

Specifically, do not add:

* AI assistant
* AI search interface
* AI Gateway
* personalized recommendations
* interest-based content
* location-based recommendations
* nearby hospitals
* map UI
* advertisements
* promotional banners
* auto-playing content
* carousels
* complex community tabs
* e-commerce functionality
* future-feature placeholders

---

## Final Goal

This should be a **refinement of the existing homepage, not a redesign**.

The result should retain the current strong information architecture while improving:

**clarity
→ hierarchy
→ page rhythm
→ accessibility
→ consistency
→ extensibility**

The homepage should feel like a trustworthy and approachable Korean community + information platform.

Use spacing, typography, alignment, and information structure to improve the experience rather than decorative visual effects.

Modify only the existing homepage in this iteration. Do not create additional pages yet.
